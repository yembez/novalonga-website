What low friction means to us

Low friction,
our line of work.

Low friction is not “simplistic.” It is a company stance: refuse complexity for its own sake. Design every screen, every gesture, every word to reduce effort — not to impress with feature density.

Today, too many tools ask you to learn a system before you can act. Stacked settings, fragmented flows, constant alerts: you end up spending more time managing the tool than living what you opened it for.

We do the opposite. Start from natural gestures. Remove what slows you down. Keep only what serves. Because a good tool barely shows itself: it leaves room for what matters — a memory, a sentence, a moment with your children.

Tools built to serve,
not to occupy.

Why now

Tools keep getting more complex.
We choose ergonomics.

The hidden cost of complexity

Every extra option has a price: attention, hesitation, drop-off. We measure a product’s success by how easy it is to enter — not by how many features we can list.

Deliberate ergonomics

At NOVALONGA, ergonomics is not a marketing add-on. It is a permanent decision criterion: if it creates friction, we redesign it or remove it.

Private by design

Less friction also means fewer unnecessary detours through your data. Privacy guides our technical choices as much as comfort of use.

Human, always

Tech should lighten life, not saturate it. Our apps exist to serve moments and relationships — then get out of the way.
